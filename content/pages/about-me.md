![Portrait of Carlos Echenique]({static}/images/about-me/me.jpg)

Carlos Echenique is a photographer with wide-ranging tastes - conceptual portraits, cinematic portraits, landscapes, and travel just to name a few. His works have been on display at the Coral Gables Museum, the Futurama Galleries, and the Miami-Dade Public Library. His photos have been published in *Art District News* and was the cover article on the December 2018 issue of *The Linux Journal.* He was an official photographer for the *International Ballet Festival*, the University of Miami Frost School of Music, as well as the artist group *7 plus 1*. He has taught photography to middle school children and adults for over five years. He holds a Bachelor's of Science in Computer Science and a Master's of Science in Management of Information Systems. He is a self-taught photographer who started at age 12.

## my kit(s)

I have settled on Fuji as my camera brand of choice and I currently sport the following two kits:

### real estate kit

My "Real Estate kit" is what I use for real estate photography. It consists of the following equipment:

- Fujifilm X-T5
- Sigma 18-50mm f/2.8 DC DN
- Sigma 10-18mm f/2.8 DC DN
- Godox AD200 + Nano Trigger F

### travel + walkabout kit

I have combined my travel and walkabout setups into one streamlined kit:

- Fujifilm GFX100RF + Tilta Vintage Travel Cage + Haoge Metal Lens Hood
- Godox iT30Pro-F mini-flash
- K&F Concepts Magnetic Filter Kit (49mm)
- Promaster Mini Carbon Fiber Tripod

This kit is packed into a [ThinkTank TurnStyle 8L Sling Bag](https://www.thinktankphoto.com/products/turnstyle-8l) along with a Framework 13 AMD laptop and all of the accessories needed for extended time away from home.
